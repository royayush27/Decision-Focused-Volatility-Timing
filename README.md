# Robin's Experiment 1 (as delivered)

Robin's original code, saved models' outputs, result tables and report (`Untitled.pdf`), unchanged.
It compares accuracy-trained (QMLE) and decision-trained (portfolio utility) GARCH, GJR, an LSTM overlay and the
Zhao et al. (2024) GARCH-LSTM on the six Fama-French factors, 5 seeds.

On `main`, `robin_experiment/` holds a copy with notes, and `9_dfl_robin.py` re-evaluates his saved forecasts under our frozen
protocol (it reproduces his reported Sharpe ratios to 0.0005).
