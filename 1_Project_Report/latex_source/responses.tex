Some answers rely on robustness checks run for these questions (alternative caps, start years, sub-samples and benchmarks) that are not reported in the main sections of the report; where a check has not been run, the answer says so.

\noindent All numbers come from the final version of our analysis. In the revised training run, notebook \texttt{lstm\_training\_revised.ipynb}, GJR-GARCH and the GARCH-LSTM are trained on the same data from 1963 as the other models, with a five-year validation block and the January reset of $c$ inside the decision loss. The portfolio results come from the final notebook, \texttt{vol\_timing\_final\_revised.ipynb} (US market 1998--2025, net of 10 bp costs). Several questions quote numbers from our presentation, which used the earlier training run; where a number changed, the answer gives the final value.
